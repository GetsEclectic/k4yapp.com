Fuel Economy Calculator Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-08-01

This Privacy Policy applies to Fuel Economy Calculator (com.k4y.fueleconomycalculator), published by K4Y Apps. It explains what data the app collects, shares, and how you can control it.

Summary

Fuel Economy Calculator stores your fill-up records and MPG history only on your device in a local database. There is no account, no cloud sync, and the app does not upload your fuel or mileage data to any server.

The app uses third-party advertising and analytics SDKs that collect some device and usage data as listed below. Each SDK's own privacy policy is linked.

Data the app collects

The app does not send any data off your device to us or to any service provider.

The following data is shared with the third-party advertising SDKs listed below, primarily so that ads can be served and measured. None of this data identifies you by name.

Your choices

  • Reset or delete your advertising ID from Settings → Privacy → Ads (or equivalent) on your device.
  • Limit ad personalization from Settings → Privacy → Ads → Opt out of Ads Personalization.
  • Uninstall the app to remove all local data created by the app from your device.

Children's privacy

Fuel Economy Calculator is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe we have, please contact us at privacy@k4yapps.com and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

If we make material changes to this privacy policy, we will update the "last updated" date above. Continued use of the app after any changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

Contact

If you have questions or concerns about this privacy policy, please contact us at privacy@k4yapps.com.
