Lexo Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-07-27

This Privacy Policy applies to Lexo (com.lexo.keyboard), published by K4Y Apps. It explains what data the app collects, shares, and how you can control it.

Summary

Lexo is a local-only keyboard: everything it does - text prediction, autocorrect, personalization, and all ML inference - runs entirely on your device. No keystrokes or typed text are ever collected, transmitted, or included in any diagnostic report. There is no account, no cloud sync, no usage analytics, and no advertising of any kind. Crash reporting is off by default: nothing is sent unless you turn on the optional "Send crash reports" setting at Settings > Advanced > Other. If you do turn it on, anonymous crash and diagnostic logs are sent to Firebase Crashlytics to help fix bugs, and you can turn it off again at any time.

Data the app collects

The following data is sent off your device by services we operate or treat as service providers. It is used only to keep the app working and to fix bugs; it is not sold, and it is not used to profile you or to track how you use the app.

Your choices

  • Turn Firebase Crashlytics off (or back on) at any time from Settings > Advanced > Other > "Send crash reports" in the app. It is off unless you turn it on, and nothing is sent while it is off.
  • Uninstall the app to remove all local data the keyboard created (keyboard personalization and learned words) from your device.

Children's privacy

Lexo is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe we have, please contact us at hello@k4yapp.com and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

If we make material changes to this privacy policy, we will update the "last updated" date above. Continued use of the app after any changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
